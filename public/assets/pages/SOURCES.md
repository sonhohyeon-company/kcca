Each was converted to WebP with sharp (quality 80, max width shown), EXIF orientation applied and
all metadata removed (the two phone photos carried GPS). No other edits.

tiles (zoom 16) with a pin and label added at the 경민대학교 campus point (37.74548, 127.02385; OSM has

# Original asset sources

All association material was sourced from the user's public site https://kcca-society.kr/ on 2026-09-15. Images were converted for delivery without creative alteration and keep their original proportions.

- logo-kcca.png, logo-mark.png: the association's brush-calligraphy logo supplied by the site owner (2026-09-30); logo-mark.png is its KCCA mark without the lettering, also used for app/icon.png and app/apple-icon.png
- pages/: images of the imweb information pages (협회소개, 인사말, 조직도, 오시는길, 자격증 안내).
- og.png (/public): link preview card for KakaoTalk and social sharing.
- Artworks, activity photos and other post images are no longer bundled here: they are imported from the imweb boards into the data directory and served from `/uploads/`.
- Noto Sans KR / Noto Serif KR: served through `next/font/google` (self-hosted at build time, `lib/fonts.ts`); SIL Open Font License in this directory.
